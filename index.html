Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version

  • Lockdown has no accounts, no payments and no ads.
  • Your tasks, lock history, allowed apps, commitment phrase and screen time stay on your phone.
  • The app sends crash reports and basic usage analytics to Google Firebase so problems can be found and fixed.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

This policy explains what information the Lockdown app for Android ("Lockdown", "the app", "we") handles, why, and what choices you have. Lockdown is made by suhan10productions.

1. Information that stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is never uploaded. This includes:

  • the tasks you name and the durations you choose;
  • your lock history, including completed and broken sessions;
  • the list of apps you allow during a lock;
  • your custom commitment phrase and app settings;
  • your screen-time figures, which are calculated on the device from Android's usage records.

You can erase your lock history at any time in Settings → Clear history. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

2. Information sent off your device

Lockdown uses two Google Firebase services. They collect the following, which Google processes on our behalf as a service provider:

Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports

If the app crashes or hits a serious error, a report is sent containing technical details such as the error and where it happened in the code, your device model, Android version, app version and a random installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix bugs.

Firebase Analytics — usage analytics

The app records general events such as app opens, screens viewed and session length, together with an app-instance identifier and, where your device provides it, the Android advertising ID. We use this only to understand how the app is used and to improve it. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not combine it with other information to identify you.

This information never includes the names of your tasks, your commitment phrase, your history or your screen-time details. All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). It is kept for a limited period under Google's standard retention settings and then deleted. Google's handling of this data is described in Firebase's privacy information.

3. Permissions and how they are used

Accessibility Service

Used for one purpose: to detect which app is in the foreground during a lock, so Lockdown can return you to the home screen if that app is blocked. Lockdown does not read screen content, record keystrokes, read passwords or text fields, or observe your interactions — window-content retrieval is disabled in the app's configuration. Nothing the service observes leaves your device.

Usage Access

Used to show your screen time on the Screen Time page and, if you keep usage alerts switched on, to notify you about heavy use. These figures are processed on your phone and never sent to us.

Notifications

Used to show the countdown while a lock is running, and for optional reminders and usage alerts, which you can turn off in the app's settings or in Android settings.

Installed apps list

Used to show the apps on your phone so you can choose which ones to allow during a lock. This list is not sent anywhere.

Battery optimisation exemption

Requested so Android does not stop the timer while your screen is off.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties, other than the Firebase processing described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on your phone).
  • Uninstalling Lockdown stops all collection and deletes everything stored on your device.
  • To ask about or request deletion of crash or analytics data, email us at the address below. See also Delete your data.

6. Children

Lockdown is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact
