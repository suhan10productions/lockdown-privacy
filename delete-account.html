Delete Your Lockdown Account

Lockdown by suhan10productions

If you'd like your Lockdown account and associated data permanently deleted, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to contactrouteops@gmail.com from the email address associated with your Lockdown account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Delete my Lockdown account".
  3. We will confirm your request and permanently delete your account within 7 business days.

What gets deleted

Your email address, password hash, and your commitment/break history are permanently removed from our database. This action cannot be undone.

What may be retained

Billing records required for tax, legal, or accounting purposes may be retained by our payment processor (Polar) for a limited period as required by law, even after your account is deleted with us.
