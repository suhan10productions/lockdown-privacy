Lockdown Privacy policy

Delete your data

Lockdown has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign out of or close. Here is how to remove everything.

Data on your phone

Your tasks, lock history, allowed apps, commitment phrase and screen-time figures are stored only on your device.

  • To clear your history: open Lockdown → Settings → Clear history.
  • To delete everything: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Lockdown → Storage → Clear storage.

Crash reports and analytics

The app sends crash reports and basic usage analytics to Google Firebase, linked only to a random app identifier — not your name or email. This data is deleted automatically after a limited period.

To ask for it to be deleted sooner, email us with the subject "Delete my Lockdown data". We will reply within 7 days.

Had an account before?

Earlier test versions of Lockdown offered an optional sign-up. If you created an account then and want it removed, email the address above from that email address and we will permanently delete it within 7 days.
